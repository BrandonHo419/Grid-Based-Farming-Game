# Credits

Grid-Based-Farming Game is Brandon Ho's (me) rebuild of my University Group Project in OOP (Object-Oriented Programming)
[GridFarmGame / MajorPracticalProjectOOP](https://github.com/BrandonHo419/GridFarmGame).
The original was a collaborative project; its contributors and history are recorded
in that repository. This rebuild does not claim sole authorship of the original and is simply just a passion rebuild to also enhance my portfolio.

No source files, sprites or audio from the original project were copied into this
rebuild. The crop, soil and character drawings are generated by `src/app/FarmArt.h`.
The melody and effect tones are synthesized by `src/app/FarmAudio.h`, without
external recordings or samples. Text uses raylib's bundled default font.
